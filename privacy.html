拾色四季 · 隐私政策

Shise — Privacy Policy

生效日期 / Effective: 2026-11-11　·　版本 / Version 1.0

简体中文 English

一句话：拾色四季在你的设备上分析照片颜色与拍摄季节，不把照片、分析结果或使用记录上传给开发者。应用没有账号系统，不含广告，也不含第三方统计或追踪 SDK。

简体中文

1. 我们是谁

拾色四季（英文名 Shise，下称“本应用”）由段哈哈的两元店（个人开发者，下称“我们”）开发并发布。本政策说明本应用如何处理你的信息。

联系邮箱：spduan@mail.ustc.edu.cn

2. 我们不收集什么

本应用不收集、不上传、不存储以下信息到我们的服务器：

  • 你的照片、视频及其缩略图
  • 照片的颜色分类结果、季节分类结果、手动修正记录
  • 你的相册结构、收藏、整理范围设置
  • 设备标识符、广告标识符（IDFA）、位置信息
  • 崩溃日志、行为埋点、使用时长等统计信息

本应用不包含任何第三方 SDK，不进行跨应用或跨网站追踪，NSPrivacyTracking 为 false。

3. 相册权限

本应用请求“照片”访问权限，用于：

  • 在你选择的相册和日期范围内显示照片
  • 在本机计算每张照片的主色与拍摄季节
  • 在你逐次确认后，把照片添加到系统相册中的专辑

你可以随时在 iOS「设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 照片 → 拾色四季」中更改或撤销授权。使用“有限访问”时，本应用只能处理你允许的那些照片。

本应用请求“添加照片”权限，仅用于保存你主动导出的海报或分享副本。本应用不会移动、覆盖或删除系统照片图库中的原图。

4. 数据保存在哪里

颜色分析结果、缓存、手动修正、应用内收藏、配色收藏、整理范围与偏好设置，全部保存在你的设备本地（应用沙盒中的本地文件与偏好设置）。

  • 卸载本应用会删除这些本地数据，但不会删除系统照片图库中的照片。
  • 本应用不提供自建的账号或跨设备同步服务。iOS 备份与设备迁移可能按系统设置处理本地数据，我们不保证换机或重装后的恢复情况。

5. iCloud 照片

若你开启了“iCloud 照片”并优化存储，显示缩略图、打开大图、分析或导出时，系统可能从 iCloud 下载原图。该过程由 iOS 与你的网络完成，下载内容不会经过我们的服务器。这会消耗流量，耗时取决于网络状况。

6. 你主动发起的分享

当你使用系统分享面板导出海报、配色卡或分享照片时，内容会交给你所选择的应用或服务，之后由该第三方的隐私政策约束，我们无法控制。本应用不会自动分享任何内容。

7. 儿童

本应用不面向儿童设计，也不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。未成年人应在监护人的指导下管理照片访问与分享。

8. 你的权利

由于本应用不向开发者传输任何个人信息，我们手中没有可供查询、导出或删除的个人数据。你在本机数据上的权利，可通过以下方式行使：

  • 删除全部分析数据：删除本应用以移除应用本地数据；「重新分析」仅重建当前范围的自动分类，不等于删除收藏或手动标记
  • 撤回相册授权：iOS 设置中随时可撤销
  • 导出或迁移：本应用数据仅存于本机，不提供云端导出

如你对本政策有疑问、投诉或行使权利的要求，请联系 spduan@mail.ustc.edu.cn，我们会根据问题内容处理并回复。

9. 国际用户（GDPR / UK GDPR / CCPA）

若你位于欧洲经济区、英国或美国加利福尼亚州：

  • 数据控制者：段哈哈的两元店，联系方式同上。
  • 处理的法律依据：本应用不在我们的服务器上处理个人数据。设备上的照片访问基于你的明示授权（同意），你可随时撤回。
  • 不出售、不共享：我们不出售、不出租、不以广告为目的共享任何个人信息（CCPA 意义上的 “sale” 与 “sharing” 均未发生）。
  • 无跨境传输：本应用不向开发者上传照片或分析结果；系统 iCloud 下载与用户主动分享见上述说明。
  • 不进行自动化决策：颜色与季节分类仅用于浏览与整理，不产生法律或类似重大影响的决定。

10. 政策变更

若本应用未来新增联网、账号、云同步或分析功能，我们会在功能上线前更新本政策，并在应用内提示。若变更涉及需要你授权的数据处理，我们会在适用情况下重新征求你的同意。

11. 本页面的技术说明

本页面不加载第三方字体、脚本或统计代码，也不主动设置 Cookie。网页托管服务可能为安全与运维记录 IP 地址、访问时间等常规日志；具体以实际托管服务的政策为准。

English

1. Who we are

Shise (拾色四季) is an iOS app developed and published by Duan Haha's Two-Yuan Shop (段哈哈的两元店), an individual developer ("we", "us"). This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Contact: spduan@mail.ustc.edu.cn

2. What we do not collect

Shise does not collect, upload, or store the following on our servers:

  • Your photos, videos, or thumbnails
  • Color or season classification results, or manual corrections
  • Your album structure, in-app favorites, or scope settings
  • Device identifiers, advertising identifiers (IDFA), or location
  • Crash logs, analytics events, or usage statistics

The app contains no third-party SDKs and performs no cross-app or cross-site tracking. NSPrivacyTracking is false.

3. Photo library access

Shise requests access to your Photo Library to:

  • Display photos within the albums and date range you choose
  • Compute each photo's dominant color and capture season on device
  • Add photos to albums in the Photos app, only after you confirm each action

You can change or revoke access at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → Shise. With limited access, the app can only process the photos you allow.

Shise requests add-only access solely to save a poster or shared copy that you export yourself. The app never moves, overwrites, or deletes originals in your library.

4. Where data is stored

Analysis results, caches, manual corrections, favorites, saved palettes, scope settings, and preferences are stored locally on your device, inside the app's sandbox, in local files and preferences.

  • Deleting the app removes this local data. It does not delete photos from your library.
  • Shise has no account or app-managed cross-device sync. iOS backup and device migration may handle local data according to your settings; restoration after reinstalling or switching devices is not guaranteed.

5. iCloud Photos

If you use iCloud Photos with optimized storage, iOS may download originals from iCloud when showing thumbnails, opening a full photo, analyzing, or exporting. That transfer is handled by iOS and your network; downloaded content does not pass through our servers. It may use cellular data, and timing depends on your connection.

6. Sharing you initiate

When you use the system share sheet to export a poster, a palette card, or a photo, the content goes to the app or service you pick and is then governed by that third party's privacy policy. Shise never shares anything automatically.

7. Children

Shise is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Minors should manage photo access and sharing with guidance from a parent or guardian.

8. Your rights

Because no personal data is transmitted to the developer, we hold nothing to look up, export, or erase on our side. You can exercise control over the data on your device by:

  • Deleting analysis data — delete the app to remove its local data. Rescan only rebuilds automatic classification for the current scope; it does not erase favorites or manual colors
  • Revoking photo access — anytime in iOS Settings
  • Exporting or porting — data lives only on your device; no cloud export is offered

For questions, complaints, or rights requests, email spduan@mail.ustc.edu.cn. We will review your request and respond as appropriate.

9. International users (GDPR / UK GDPR / CCPA)

If you are in the EEA, the UK, or California:

  • Data controller: Duan Haha's Two-Yuan Shop, contact above.
  • Legal basis: We do not process personal data on our servers. On-device photo access is based on your explicit permission, which you can withdraw at any time.
  • No sale or sharing: We do not sell, rent, or share personal information for advertising. No "sale" or "sharing" under the CCPA occurs.
  • No cross-border transfer: No photos or analysis results are uploaded to the developer. System-managed iCloud transfers and sharing you initiate are described above.
  • No automated decision-making: Color and season labels are used only for browsing and organizing and produce no legal or similarly significant effects.

10. Changes to this policy

If a future version adds networking, accounts, cloud sync, or analytics, we will update this policy and notify you in the app before that feature ships. Where a change requires authorization for data processing, we will seek your consent again when applicable.

11. About this page

This page loads no third-party fonts, scripts, or analytics and does not set cookies itself. The hosting provider may keep standard logs such as IP addresses and access times for security and operations, subject to its own policy.
